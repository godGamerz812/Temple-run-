# Code of Conduct

## Our promise
We want participation in Sky Run – Dino Chase to be a welcoming, respectful experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior
- Be kind, patient, and constructive.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect different experience levels and viewpoints.
- Accept reasonable corrections and decisions made to protect the project.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting
Report concerns privately to the repository owner or a maintainer through GitHub. Include relevant links or context when safe to do so. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe.
